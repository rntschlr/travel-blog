The sakura season has officially begun! Kyoto has transformed into a pink wonderland.

## Philosopher's Path

I walked the famous Philosopher's Path today - a canal lined with hundreds of cherry trees. The petals were falling like snow, creating a pink carpet along the water. It's one of those moments that makes you stop and just breathe.

## Hanami Parties

The locals celebrate hanami - flower viewing parties - under the trees. I was invited to join a group of university students who shared their bento boxes and sake with me. Language barriers melt away when you're sharing food and appreciating beauty together.

## Night Viewing

At night, the temples do special illumination events. The combination of spotlights and cherry blossoms against the dark sky is surreal. I'll try to get some good photos to share.

The beauty here is almost overwhelming. Can't believe I almost didn't come during this season!
