I finally made it to Kyoto! After a long flight from San Francisco, I'm settling into my small but cozy ryokan near Gion district.

## The Golden Pavilion

My first stop was Kinkaku-ji, the famous Golden Pavilion. The sight of the gold-leaf covered temple reflected in the mirror pond was absolutely breathtaking. I spent over an hour just sitting by the water, watching the light change.

## Tea Ceremony Experience

Yesterday I had my first traditional tea ceremony. The precision and mindfulness of the whole experience was humbling. The host explained each movement has meaning - from the way the tea bowl is turned to the specific fold of the cloth.

## Food Adventures

The food here is incredible. I've been eating ramen almost every day (no complaints!) and yesterday tried kaiseki - a traditional multi-course meal. Each dish was like a work of art.
