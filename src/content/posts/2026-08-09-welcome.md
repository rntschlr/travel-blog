Hey everyone!

This little corner of the internet is now live. No algorithms, no ads, no
social media accounts needed — just a simple place where I'll post stories and
photos from the road.

## How to follow along

- **Email** — there's a subscribe box on the homepage; pop your address in
  and new posts land in your inbox. (If it says "coming soon", the email list
  is still being hooked up — check back shortly, or use the RSS feed.)
- **Bookmark** — or just save this page and check back whenever you're curious.

## What's coming

Stories, photos, food I probably shouldn't have eaten, and the occasional
wrong turn that turned out to be the best part of the day.
